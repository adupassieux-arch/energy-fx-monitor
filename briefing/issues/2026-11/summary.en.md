<p class="kicker">Eight months into the Iran war, oil is priced for scarcity, not for a deal. Brent spot stood at ${{brent.last}} on {{brent.last_date}}, {{brent.chg_ytd}} since January, and its volatility is higher than {{volrank.brent}} of readings since 2021. Gas is plentiful in the United States and scarce in Asia. The dollar is gaining against the euro and losing against the yuan.</p>

### The month in brief

- **Crude stays above $100.** Brent is far above its pre-war level of $71.32. Tanker attacks around Hormuz and, on 22 October, on Saudi territory keep a risk premium in place.
- **Physical tighter than paper.** On 20 October, Brent spot settled about $6 above the December futures contract.
- **OPEC+ cannot add much.** November quotas are unchanged, and Saudi, Iraqi and Kuwaiti output is still below pre-war levels.
- **Gas splits in two.** Henry Hub is at ${{henry_hub.last}} per MMBtu; Asian LNG (JKM) reached $32.20 on 26 October, about ten times more.
- **Dollar up against the euro, down against the yuan.** EUR/USD is at a 52-week low of {{eurusd.last}}; USD/CNY at {{usdcny.last}} reflects the yuan's strongest level since mid-2022.
