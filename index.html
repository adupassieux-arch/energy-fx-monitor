    Method & sources
    Crude oil & gas
    Brent and WTI spot (USD/bbl), Henry Hub spot (USD/MMBtu), U.S. Energy Information Administration via FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.
    Exchange rates
    European Central Bank euro reference rates. USD/CNY is derived from the ECB's CNY and USD rates against the euro.
    Volatility
    Standard deviation of daily log returns over 30 trading days, multiplied by √252.
    Correlation
    Pearson correlation of daily log returns, on days where all five markets published a price.
    One-month range
    Driftless random walk: the central line is today's price, bands use the last 60 days of volatility (80% and 95%). Tested weekly over the last three years against the realised price 20 trading days later.
    Update
    Rebuilt automatically every week from the public sources above.
